**Method**

Exhibit three starts from the same backend evidence through a third self-contained application. The first implementation proves direct routes, collection and detail reads, event-driven refresh, stored logs, and live tails without borrowing a shell, component, token, or style from either neighboring exhibit.

Its neutral state is deliberate. A scaffold should make a broken premise visible and make real data easy to inspect; it should not spend the strongest visual choices before the exhibit has a method worth testing.

The DAG response is read and counted as part of the contract, then left undesigned. Choosing a graph, tree, timeline, or other visual account belongs to the actual cut and must follow its argument rather than arrive as framework furniture.
