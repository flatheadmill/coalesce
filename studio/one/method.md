**Method**

Exhibit one begins by proving custody of the complete Coalesce contract before making a visual argument. Its initial app keeps the record legible, names every state in text, and leaves the DAG unpictured while still acknowledging that the response exists.

The implementation belongs wholly to this exhibit. It may read the shared transport and fixtures, but its route components, state transitions, markup, language, and CSS stay here so later judgments can change without moving either sibling.

The first cut has not begun. This neutral scaffold is the instrument used to inspect live data, narrow and wide routes, completion transitions, missing evidence, and transport failures before the exhibit chooses what deserves emphasis.

**Sources**

`cmd/web/main.go` is the authority for response shapes, route paths, and WebSocket behavior.

`ui/src/api.ts` records the existing product UI's normalization of an empty runs response and its current container-name convention.
